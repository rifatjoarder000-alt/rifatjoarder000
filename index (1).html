Keyframe vs Physics animation

Same room, same ball. On the left, every moment of the ball’s motion is authored by hand. On the right, the motion comes from gravity and friction being calculated live.

The big idea: who decides the motion?

Each method gets its motion from a different source. Watch how.

Keyframe: a script

You place the key poses. The computer fills in the frames between them.

Physics: the rules of a game

You set the rules. Gravity, bounce and friction decide what happens.

Motion capture: a recorded actor

A real person moves. The movement is copied onto a character.
